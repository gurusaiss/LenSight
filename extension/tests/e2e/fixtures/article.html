Why Local-First AI Assistants Matter

Local-first software keeps your data on your own device by default, giving you control over what leaves your machine and when. This matters most for AI assistants that read sensitive content like messages, documents, and personal notes.

Privacy By Architecture

Rather than trusting a policy promise, a local-first assistant is architected so that extraction, chunking, and embedding all happen on-device before any network call is made. Only the minimum retrieved context is ever sent to a remote AI provider, and only if the user explicitly chooses one.

This approach also means the assistant keeps working offline, in a degraded but honest "basic local mode" that returns the most relevant extracted excerpts instead of a generated answer when no AI provider is configured.
